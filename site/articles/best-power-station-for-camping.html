Best power station for camping

Direct answer: On cost per watt-hour, EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro leads the portable power stations we track at $0.74/Wh ($569.00 from Shop Solar Kits). It weighs 17.2 lb. For camping the ranking is only half the question: the other half is what you are willing to lift, and that trade-off is set out below.

By Brandon Hall · Prices on this page refreshed when the site was last built; price record runs from 2026-08-13 to 2026-09-04.

Camping is the use case where the cheapest watt-hour is most often the wrong answer. Every extra watt-hour is extra mass, and mass is the constraint that actually bites when the thing has to come out of a car and go somewhere.

Helios has not physically tested any product on this page. Our authority is price data, published specifications and our own scrape history — that is a narrower claim than a review site makes, and it is the one we can actually support.

The weight trade-off

Below is every weight our catalog actually stores for these products. It is not a complete set, and the gaps are shown as gaps: we record a weight when a tracked listing states one, and we do not estimate the rest.

Specification EcoFlow RIVER 3EcoFlow RIVER 2 ProBluetti AC180EcoFlow DELTA Max 2000Bluetti AC200LAnker SOLIX F2600
Usable capacity 245 Wh 768 Wh not published 2,016 Wh 2,048 Wh 2,560 Wh
Weight not published 17.2 lb 35.3 lb 48 lb 62.4 lb 67.2 lb
Rated output 300 W 800 W 1,800 W 2,400 W 2,400 W 2,400 W

Read that table against the ranking above and the shape of the decision appears: the cheapest energy per watt-hour generally sits in the larger, heavier units, because the fixed cost of the inverter, case and electronics is spread over more cells. A small station is nearly always worse value per watt-hour and better value per kilogram carried.

What we cannot tell you is how any of these behave in a tent at 2 a.m. — fan noise, cold-weather charge behaviour, how the app copes without signal, whether the handle is comfortable after two hundred metres. We have not used them. Where a review site would give you an opinion, we give you the price history and the published numbers, and you should read an owner's account for the rest.
