Affiliate disclosure

Generated from the current data on 2026-08-14.

The short version

Helios earns nothing today. It is not enrolled in any retailer's affiliate programme, no outbound link on this site carries a tracking parameter, and no commission is being paid on anything you click. The intention is to join programmes later, and this page is written so it describes the arrangement accurately once that happens.

Outbound links are nevertheless already marked rel="nofollow sponsored" in the HTML. That is deliberate: marking a link as commercial before it becomes commercial is harmless, whereas forgetting to mark it afterwards is the failure that matters. Treat every retailer link here as one Helios may eventually earn from.

Why the ranking cannot be bought

  • Rankings are computed, not curated. Every ranked list on this site is sorted by a number derived from scraped prices at build time. There is no editorial ordering step and no priority field a retailer could pay into.
  • The cheapest price wins even when it earns nothing. Because commission is not an input to any calculation, a retailer with no programme outranks one with a programme whenever its price is lower.
  • The comparison is the product. A tracker that steers readers toward a worse price for a better commission is worth nothing to a reader, and a tracker worth nothing to readers earns nothing either. The incentive only points one way over any useful time horizon.
  • You can check. The code that ranks these pages and the price history it ranks from are both public at https://github.com/ProjectGaiaA/Helios.

What the prices do and do not include

Displayed prices are the retailer's own listed price for their own listed variant at the moment it was last read, shown with that timestamp. They exclude shipping, tax, installation, and any rebate or incentive. Prices older than 168 hours are withheld rather than displayed. A retailer can change a price at any time after a check, so the price on their site at checkout is the only one that binds.
